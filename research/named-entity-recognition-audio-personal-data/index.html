SBSEG 2025 · 2025

Evaluation of Named Entity Recognition for Personal Data Discovery in Audio Transcriptions

A study on using fine-tuned BERTimbau to identify personal data in audio transcripts and extract entity relationships.

Carlos André Misiuk Munhos · Luciano Ignaczak

This paper evaluates a fine-tuned BERTimbau model using a corpus based on synthetic data to identify names, CPF and RG numbers, and addresses in audio transcripts. It also tests extracting the relations “Resides in” and “Holds document.”

The model reached an F1 score of 0.98 for named entity recognition and 0.29 for relation extraction. The paper was published in the extended proceedings of SBSeg 2025.
